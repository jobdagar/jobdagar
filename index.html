Your Safety Matters

Follow these simple steps while searching and applying for jobs online.

🛡️

Protect Your Account

Never share your password, OTP or login information with another person. Use a strong password for your account.

🔎

Verify Job Details

Check the company name, job description, location and other details before submitting an application.

💰

Never Pay for a Job

Be careful if someone asks for money, fees or sensitive financial information in exchange for employment.

Important: JobDagar is a platform for discovering job opportunities. Always independently verify an employer and job offer before sharing sensitive information or making any payment.
